# LSA Sales Accelerator: Cohort Package (DRAFT v1)

> Assumptions to confirm are marked **[CONFIRM]**. Nothing here is final until pricing and capacity are validated.

## Offer in one line
A once-per-term cohort (Fall + Winter) where employers join the LSA Sales Accelerator for **$500** and get Winternship access plus real exposure to Laurier's campus. An optional **Laurier Engagement System** is available as a **$2,000** upgrade.

## Who pays **[CONFIRM]**
Businesses/employers hosting or sponsoring Winternship talent. If the buyer is anyone else (students, clubs), the messaging below changes.

## Tier 1: Cohort Seat: $500
Included in the cohort:
- **Winternship access**: placement/matching with Laurier students for the term
- **Campus exposure**: presence at campus touchpoints (events, tabling, class/club visibility) **[CONFIRM what LSA can actually deliver]**
- **In-office tour**: a student/cohort visit to the employer's office for an employer-brand moment
- **Social media awareness**: baseline feature across LSA channels (e.g., cohort announcement + 1-2 posts) **[CONFIRM channels and follower reach]**
- Cohort membership: peer network with the other companies, start-of-term kickoff, end-of-term showcase

## Tier 2: Laurier Engagement System: $2,000 (add-on to a Cohort Seat)
A full campaign to build the employer's brand and pipeline with Laurier students:
- Campus campaign calendar for the term (events, activations, content)
- Dedicated social content package (defined number of posts/reels/stories)
- Student ambassador or campus-rep program **[CONFIRM]**
- Lead capture + follow-up (e.g., sign-up form, CRM list of interested students)
- Mid-term and end-of-term results report (reach, sign-ups, applicants)

*Rule: Tier 2 requires Tier 1. Total = $2,500 per term.*

## Open questions that drive the numbers
1. **Capacity**: how many companies per cohort? Revenue at 10 seats = $5k (Tier 1 only); at 10 seats with 3 upgrades = $11k.
2. **Cost per seat**: what does one seat cost you in hours, tour logistics, and content? $500 must clear that with margin.
3. **Is the $2k Engagement System defined and deliverable?** Selling an unscoped system is the biggest risk. What are the exact deliverables?
4. **Upgrade attach rate target**: what percent of Tier 1 buyers should convert to Tier 2? If low, add a $1,000 mid-tier (e.g., campus campaign only, no ambassador program).
5. **Proof**: what outcomes can you show from past cohorts (placements, reach)? Without it, $2k is a hard sell to a first-time buyer.
